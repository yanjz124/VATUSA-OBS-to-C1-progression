Compare a controller

Enter a CID (or a name) to compare each stage against that controller's facility and the whole division. Stages without a record are skipped, and a stage still in progress shows the time so far.

Method. Home rosters are fetched live in your browser from api.vatusa.net/v2/facility/<id>/roster/home for every active ARTCC. Controllers rated C1 or higher (C1, C3, I1, I3, SUP, ADM) are kept. Duration = date(S3→C1) − date(OBS→S1), taken from each member's promotion log. Controllers missing either promotion (usually transfers from outside VATUSA or incomplete legacy records) are omitted.

Results are cached in your browser for one hour. Not affiliated with VATSIM or VATUSA.
